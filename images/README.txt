Drop your Squidward grave tribute GIF in this folder and name it:

    squidward-grave.gif

That's the filename the site looks for (set in data.js -> GRAVEYARD_GIF).
It'll appear at the top of the Graveyard section automatically.

Use a different name or path? Just update GRAVEYARD_GIF in data.js to match.
Don't want a GIF at all? Set GRAVEYARD_GIF = "" in data.js.
